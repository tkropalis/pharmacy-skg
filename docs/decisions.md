No summer schedule is applied until one is verified for the year. A duty day counts as published for a pharmacy only when that day's file contains the pharmacy's area group. Agreed 5 Oct 2026. |

## Defaults

These were chosen without a formal decision and apply until someone objects.

- **Status labels:** everyday words, no source names and no parentheses (the owner, 5 Oct 2026). A pharmacy on a published duty list shows "Εφημερεύει" / "On duty". One open by its regular hours or by its ΠΚΜ extended-hours schedule shows "Ανοιχτό" / "Open": "διευρυμένο ωράριο" was too complicated, and to the person both simply mean open. The row details add the closing time in one sentence ("Ανοιχτό έως 21:00", "Εφημερεύει όλη τη νύχτα έως αύριο 08:00" for an after-midnight duty), and count down only in the last 30 minutes ("Ανοιχτό, κλείνει σε 25 λεπτά, στις 21:00"). An on-duty pharmacy whose list prints no hours shows "Εφημερεύει, καλέστε για το ωράριο". The words are defined once (`apps/web/src/i18n/status-labels.ts`) and the list, the row details and every page use them. Status is never shown by colour alone: every status has words, and on the map each kind keeps its own marker shape (duty a square with a cross, regular a dot, extended a diamond), which the legend explains in plain words ("Ανοιχτό, συνηθισμένο ωράριο", "Ανοιχτό, περισσότερες ώρες από τα άλλα").
- **Plain words:** every word on screen should be understood at once by an older person who has never heard of ΦΣΘ or ΠΚΜ: no abbreviations, administrative terms or parentheses in labels (the owner, 5 Oct 2026). Sources are still credited, by their full names: the home sheet's footer says "Οι εφημερίες είναι από τον Φαρμακευτικό Σύλλογο Θεσσαλονίκης", the other pages' footer names the association and the Region of Central Macedonia, and the about page lists every source with its licence (thess.guide included). Freshness stays visible as plain words, "Ενημερώθηκε 07:46", linking to the credits. A unit test fails if ΦΣΘ or ΠΚΜ appears in any UI string.
- **Stale data:** if the newest data is older than 36 hours, show a warning banner. Never hide the data silently.
- **Privacy:** the user's location and medicine searches never leave the device. Distances are computed on the device. The app asks the browser for the position when it opens (the browser asks the person first) and follows it while the page is visible; it does not ask when the person chose an area, cleared the position, or the browser has the permission blocked. Only those choices are stored, as flags, never coordinates. Agreed 5 Oct 2026.
- **Look:** one light theme on every device, whatever the system setting (`color-scheme: light`; the owner's choice, 5 Oct 2026), with a pharmacy-green accent (#0a7d45) and soft tints for the status colours. The typeface is Commissioner (Kostas Bartsokas, Thessaloniki; SIL OFL), self-hosted with its Greek and Latin subsets and a little "flare". Every icon is Font Awesome Free (CC BY 4.0, credited on the about page), drawn as inline SVG from the icon's path (`components/app/icons.tsx`, `lib/fa.ts`), including the map's markers and controls. The base map is OpenFreeMap's Positron, a pale style the markers stand out on. Accessibility target: WCAG 2.2 AA.
- **Legal:** a disclaimer ("call before you go; not medical advice"), a privacy page, and credits for every data source.
- **Name:** "pharmacy-skg" is a working title. The public name and domain are chosen before launch.
- **Home layout:** the home screen is an app viewport (D8): it fills exactly the visible screen (`100dvh`, safe areas respected), the page does not scroll, only the sheet's list does, and there is no page footer on it (the sheet ends with a compact one: links, the emergency numbers as tel: links, the disclaimer and the source). Only the header (one row) sits above the map: there is no emergency strip, on any page; the owner found it alarming (5 Oct 2026), and the numbers stay in the sheet's footer and on the about page. By day, with no position, the list starts with a "see the nearest" card (location button and area picker). The sheet's header holds the tabs, one line with the count and when the data was updated ("Ενημερώθηκε 07:46"), and one toolbar row (the origin chip and the "All / On duty" filter). The list is a plain list divided by hairlines, not cards, and shows only what decides the choice: the name, a short status in its colour ("Εφημερεύει", "Ανοιχτό") with the closing time, and the distance and address, with round Call and Directions buttons (Apple Maps on Apple devices, Google Maps elsewhere). Tapping a row shows it on the map and opens its details: the status in one sentence, the directions apps ("Οδηγίες με:"), favourite, share ("Αποστολή"), the pharmacy's page ("Περισσότερα") and "Αναφορά λάθους". The origin chip reads "Κοντά μου" when distances are measured from the person's position. Revised three times on 5 Oct 2026 after the owner's iPhone reviews.
- **Map:** MapLibre GL JS with OpenFreeMap tiles and Greek labels. Pharmacies on duty (and those whose list prints no hours) are never clustered and are drawn above everything else, as the only large markers (a square with a cross); regular hours are small slate dots and extended hours small purple diamonds, kept apart from the blue position dot. Names show from zoom 14.5 for duty pharmacies and only from zoom 17 for the rest; only regular and extended-hours pharmacies are clustered, with small, muted clusters. It starts once the page has settled after the list (about 1–2 s after the list), or at once when touched, so that starting it does not block the first reading and scrolling (apps/web/README.md, Lighthouse).
- **Directions:** deep links into Google Maps, Apple Maps or Waze, plus a call link. No routing API.
- **Geocoding:** once per address, with Nominatim. Results are stored with their source and a confidence level, and manual corrections live in the repo. Google geocoding is not used, because of its terms.
- **Tooling:**
  - pnpm workspaces; Vitest; ESLint 10 and Prettier; Playwright later.
  - Strict TypeScript 6. Version 7 is not yet supported by typescript-eslint or `astro check`.
  - Node 22, running `.ts` files natively.

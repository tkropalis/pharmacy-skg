# Decisions

Agreed during the planning rounds on 4 Oct 2026. To change a decision, edit this file in the same PR that changes the behaviour.

## Product

| #   | Topic                 | Decision                                                                                                                                                    | Why                                                                                                                                             |
| --- | --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| D1  | Goal                  | A free, ad-free public app that becomes the default way to find an open pharmacy in Greece, starting with Thessaloniki (the owner, 6 Oct 2026: D26).        | Trust and speed matter more than revenue.                                                                                                       |
| D2  | ΦΣΘ                   | Build on ΦΣΘ's public duty lists, with attribution. Contact them by email once a working beta exists; Claude drafts the email and a one-page brief.         | Their permission or an official feed removes the biggest risk, and a working demo persuades better than a pitch.                                |
| D3  | Platform              | An installable web app (PWA) first. A native wrapper only if it gets traction.                                                                              | Shareable links, search-engine traffic and the fastest path to launch.                                                                          |
| D4  | What counts as "open" | Every pharmacy at any hour: regular hours, summer hours, extended hours, every duty type and holidays.                                                      | That is the question people actually ask. Competitors only list duty pharmacies.                                                                |
| D5  | Geography             | The whole Thessaloniki regional unit first, then the rest of Greece (D26). Data and URLs carry a city key, one per pharmacists' association's area.         | Thessaloniki first; one shared platform (ITeQ) carries most other areas' lists, and Larissa is on it, not on ΦΣΘ's system (research-greece.md). |
| D6  | Prices                | In v1.1, medicine lookup: official prescription price and a shortage warning (the co-payment estimate was dropped, see D24). The search runs on the device. | Prescription prices are the same everywhere, non-prescription prices are only indicative, and stock data is not public.                         |
| D7  | Languages             | Greek (default) and English, built so more languages can be added.                                                                                          | Locals, students and visitors.                                                                                                                  |
| D8  | Home screen           | A map, with a bottom sheet listing the nearest open pharmacies. Each row has call and directions buttons.                                                   | A familiar pattern that works one-handed.                                                                                                       |
| D9  | Hospitals             | On-duty hospitals are out of scope.                                                                                                                         | Focus.                                                                                                                                          |
| D10 | Favourites            | Saved on the device, no accounts. Show each favourite's officially published next duty dates, with add-to-calendar. No push notifications.                  | Useful without a backend.                                                                                                                       |
| D11 | Duty forecasts        | Show only officially published duty dates. The rotation may be reconstructed internally, but forecasts are published only if ΦΣΘ agrees.                    | We should not antagonise the people we want a data feed from.                                                                                   |

| D23 | Open-now rules      | A pharmacy is open when any of these applies, all in Europe/Athens local time: (1) regular hours, Mon/Wed 08:00–14:30 and Tue/Thu/Fri 08:00–14:00 and 17:00–21:00 (ΠΚΜ, since 23 Mar 2021), none on weekends or holidays; (2) its extended-hours schedule (ΠΚΜ's, or ΦΣΑ's in Attica, D27), which replaces the regular hours on each day it lists (weekly schedules don't apply on holidays, dated ones do); (3) every published duty section listing it, with that heading's hours and extra hours. A holiday is a national holiday (fixed dates and those that move with Orthodox Easter), 26 Oct for the metro group, or a weekday whose duty list uses a 'Σάββατο, Κυριακή και αργίες' heading. A duty heading without hours gives the status 'on duty, hours not stated', for the duty day (08:00 to 08:00 the next morning) that contains the moment asked about. Μεγάλη Παρασκευή and Αγίου Πνεύματος are holidays only until the group's duty list for that date is published: once it is, the list decides (a holiday only with an 'αργίες' heading), while the other national holidays and 26 Oct stay calendar-authoritative. No summer schedule is applied until one is verified for the year. A duty day counts as published for a pharmacy only when that day's file contains the pharmacy's area group. Agreed 5 Oct 2026. ## Defaults

These were chosen without a formal decision and apply until someone objects.

- **Status labels:** everyday words, no source names and no parentheses (the owner, 5 Oct 2026). A pharmacy on a published duty list shows "Εφημερεύει" / "On duty". One open by its regular hours or by its ΠΚΜ extended-hours schedule shows "Ανοιχτό" / "Open": "διευρυμένο ωράριο" was too complicated, and to the person both simply mean open. The row's status line gives the closing time with its day when it is not today ("έως 21:00", "έως αύριο 08:00"), says "έως τα μεσάνυχτα" / "until midnight" for an end at 00:00 (never "έως αύριο 00:00"), and counts down only in the last 30 minutes ("κλείνει σε 25 λεπτά"). An on-duty pharmacy whose list prints no hours shows "Εφημερεύει, καλέστε για το ωράριο"; an on-call one (D26) "Εφημερεύει, καλέστε πρώτα" / "On duty, call first". Where a city's regular hours are not known (D26), a pharmacy not on duty is "Δεν εφημερεύει" / "Not on duty", never "Κλειστό", and its page says "Καλέστε για το ωράριο." under the usual hours (the owner, 6 Oct 2026). The words are defined once (`apps/web/src/i18n/status-labels.ts`) and the list, the row details and every page use them. So are the kinds of duty, in plain words instead of the printed headings: "Εφημερία ημέρας", "Εφημερία Σαββάτου", "Εφημερία", "Νυχτερινή εφημερία" (Διανυκτερεύοντα: 21:00–00:00 in the city, to 08:00 in some groups) and "Εφημερία όλη τη νύχτα" (Μεταμεσονύκτια, which start at 21:00, so never "after midnight"); the hours always follow the label (the owner, 5 Oct 2026). Status is never shown by colour alone: every status has words, and every pharmacy's marker is a rounded square with a cross, like the green cross outside every Greek pharmacy, that differs by size, body and glyph (the owner, 6 Oct 2026, replacing the dots and diamonds, which did not read as pharmacies and looked like the blue position dot): on duty large and solid green with a white cross; on duty with no hours printed the same with a "?" instead of the cross; open, by regular or extended hours alike, smaller and white with a green cross; closed small, grey and faded. Regular and extended hours share one marker, as they share one word. There is no orange: on duty with no hours printed has the duty green, in the list too (the owner, 6 Oct 2026). The legend uses the status words. When a closed pharmacy opens: Next opening.
- **Plain words:** every word on screen should be understood at once by an older person who has never heard of ΦΣΘ or ΠΚΜ: no abbreviations, administrative terms or parentheses in labels (the owner, 5 Oct 2026). Sources are credited by their full names on the about page, with every licence (thess.guide included); the footers no longer name them, because people do not need to know where the data comes from to use it (the owner, 5 Oct 2026). Freshness stays visible as plain words, "Ενημερώθηκε 07:46", which links to those credits. Copy is as short as it can be: one sentence per warning, no explanations of how the app works on its screens. A unit test fails if ΦΣΘ or ΠΚΜ appears in any UI string. Copy rules from the copy audit (the owner, 5 Oct 2026):
  - No em dashes (a unit test fails on one in any UI string) and no exclamation marks. Time ranges keep the en dash, unspaced ("08:00–14:00").
  - No sentence that restates its heading. No "only officially published" notes, source lines or "call before you go" repeats on the pages: D11 is explained once, on the about page, and "Καλέστε πριν πάτε" is in every footer.
  - One word for one thing:
    - "εφημερία/εφημερεύει" for duty.
    - "ανακοινώθηκε / announced", never "δημοσιευμένη".
    - "έως / until", never "μέχρι".
    - "μέρα", not "ημέρα" (except the set phrase "Εφημερία ημέρας").
    - "τοποθεσία" for the person's location, "θέση" only for a pharmacy's position on the map.
    - "συσκευή / device", never "κινητό".
    - "Καλέστε πριν πάτε. / Call before you go."
    - "καλέστε για το ωράριο / call for the hours" for a duty without printed hours.
    - "Ελέγξτε τη σύνδεση. / Check your connection." for network errors.
    - "Κλείσιμο / Close".
    - "Κοινοποίηση" for share, "Αποστολή" only for sending a report.
  - Accessible names are "Label: {name}" ("Κλήση: {name}", "Κοινοποίηση: {name}"): the pharmacy names are surnames in the nominative, which an article ("στο", "του") would not agree with.
  - Group names: the metro group shows as "Θεσσαλονίκη", not its printed administrative name "Πολεοδομικό Συγκρότημα Θεσσαλονίκης" (`groupDisplayName`, `apps/web/src/lib/groups.ts`).
  - Greek distances are written "μ" and "χλμ".
  - Phone numbers have a space after the area code ("2310 023026").
- **Stale data:** if the sources were last checked more than 36 hours ago, show a warning banner. Never hide the data silently. The age shown ("Ενημερώθηκε") and the banner use `checkedAt` in `meta.json`, which every successful run writes whether or not anything changed: an association that publishes a week of duties at once is not stale in between, while a run that fails or does not happen still shows the banner after 36 hours (the owner, 7 Oct 2026; until then the banner used the last change and showed for current data). A run that finds no duty list for today writes what it has but keeps the previous `checkedAt` and fails, so a source that goes quiet shows the banner too (24 Oct 2026: thess.guide uploaded nothing from 6 to 16 Oct and the app looked current with an empty list). For the areas whose sites show only today, a list repeated from the day before counts as none (D28). `updatedAt`, the last change, stays for the sitemap and the pages' no-JavaScript footer. In the home screen too, where regular hours are unknown a pharmacy off the duty list reads "Δεν εφημερεύει, επόμενη εφημερία …", never "Κλειστό", and the legend's grey marker says "Δεν εφημερεύει".
- **The chosen pharmacy:** one choice at a time, shown the same way on the row and on the map: the brand's tint with a fine edge on the row; on the map a lifted drop in the pharmacy's colour with a white edge and a small shadow where it meets the ground, a flag above it with the short name and how long it stays open, and the other pins stepping back. Choosing on the map lowers the sheet to a card (name, status, distance, "Κλήση", "Οδηγίες"); pulling it up opens the list at that row, and the cross, a tap on the empty map or Escape let it go. The camera moves only when the pharmacy is not already in the free part of the map, and then shows the origin too when it is close (within 3 km). A chosen area is a ring with its name, not the blue "you are here" dot. Replaced the ring-around-the-pin selection (the owner: "too old, too boring, bad UX", 5 Oct 2026).
- **Map:** MapLibre GL JS with OpenFreeMap tiles (Positron, or its dark style in the dark look) and Greek labels. Pharmacies on duty (and those whose list prints no hours) are never clustered and are drawn above everything else, as the only large, solid markers; the open ones are smaller white squares with a green cross (Defaults, Status labels), none of them round, so nothing looks like the blue position dot. Under the zoom buttons, a locate button, an icon only, named "Η τοποθεσία μου" (the owner, 7 Oct 2026): it asks for the position, then chooses the nearest open pharmacy as a tap on its marker would, the map showing the person and the pharmacy together however far apart they are, and on a phone the sheet lowers to its card with "Κλήση" and "Οδηγίες". With nothing open to choose, or outside every covered area, it shows only the person; a refusal is said in a toast, since the list's own message may be under the sheet. Names show from zoom 14.5 for duty pharmacies and only from zoom 17 for the rest; only regular and extended-hours pharmacies are clustered, with small, muted clusters. It starts once the page has settled after the list (about 1–2 s after the list), or at once when touched, so that starting it does not block the first reading and scrolling (apps/web/README.md, Lighthouse).
- **Directions:** deep links into Google Maps, Apple Maps or Waze, plus a call link. No routing API and no travel mode: the maps app keeps the mode the person last used (most people drive at night). The one-tap button opens the app last chosen in a row's details (remembered on the device), otherwise Apple Maps on Apple devices and Google Maps elsewhere.
- **Geocoding:** once per address, with Nominatim. Results are stored with their source and a confidence level, and manual corrections live in the repo. Google geocoding is not used, because of its terms.
- **Tooling:**
  - pnpm workspaces; Vitest; ESLint 10 and Prettier; Playwright later.
  - Strict TypeScript 6. Version 7 is not yet supported by typescript-eslint or `astro check`.
  - Node 22, running `.ts` files natively.

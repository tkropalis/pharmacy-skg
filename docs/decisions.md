# Decisions

Agreed during the planning rounds on 4 Oct 2026. To change a decision, edit this file in the same PR that changes the behaviour.

| D25 | Offline and install | Following D3. **Offline:** after one visit the app opens without a connection: the shell is precached, and the service worker keeps the data for yesterday to three days ahead (only officially published days, D11) and the last 60 pages visited. ## Defaults

These were chosen without a formal decision and apply until someone objects.

- **Status labels:** everyday words, no source names and no parentheses (the owner, 5 Oct 2026). A pharmacy on a published duty list shows "Εφημερεύει" / "On duty". One open by its regular hours or by its ΠΚΜ extended-hours schedule shows "Ανοιχτό" / "Open": "διευρυμένο ωράριο" was too complicated, and to the person both simply mean open. The row's status line gives the closing time with its day when it is not today ("έως 21:00", "έως αύριο 08:00"), says "έως τα μεσάνυχτα" / "until midnight" for an end at 00:00 (never "έως αύριο 00:00"), and counts down only in the last 30 minutes ("κλείνει σε 25 λεπτά"). An on-duty pharmacy whose list prints no hours shows "Εφημερεύει, καλέστε για το ωράριο". The words are defined once (`apps/web/src/i18n/status-labels.ts`) and the list, the row details and every page use them. So are the kinds of duty, in plain words instead of the printed headings: "Εφημερία ημέρας", "Εφημερία Σαββάτου", "Εφημερία", "Νυχτερινή εφημερία" (Διανυκτερεύοντα: 21:00–00:00 in the city, to 08:00 in some groups) and "Εφημερία όλη τη νύχτα" (Μεταμεσονύκτια, which start at 21:00, so never "after midnight"); the hours always follow the label (the owner, 5 Oct 2026). Status is never shown by colour alone: every status has words, and on the map each kind keeps its own marker shape (duty a square with a cross, regular a dot, extended a diamond), which the legend explains in plain words ("Ανοιχτό, συνηθισμένο ωράριο", "Ανοιχτό, περισσότερες ώρες από τα άλλα").
- **Plain words:** every word on screen should be understood at once by an older person who has never heard of ΦΣΘ or ΠΚΜ: no abbreviations, administrative terms or parentheses in labels (the owner, 5 Oct 2026). Sources are credited by their full names on the about page, with every licence (thess.guide included); the footers no longer name them, because people do not need to know where the data comes from to use it (the owner, 5 Oct 2026). Freshness stays visible as plain words, "Ενημερώθηκε 07:46", which links to those credits. Copy is as short as it can be: one sentence per warning, no explanations of how the app works on its screens. A unit test fails if ΦΣΘ or ΠΚΜ appears in any UI string. Copy rules from the copy audit (the owner, 5 Oct 2026):
  - No em dashes (a unit test fails on one in any UI string) and no exclamation marks. Time ranges keep the en dash, unspaced ("08:00–14:00").
  - No sentence that restates its heading. No "only officially published" notes, source lines or "call before you go" repeats on the pages: D11 is explained once, on the about page, and "Καλέστε πριν πάτε" is in every footer.
  - One word for one thing:
    - "εφημερία/εφημερεύει" for duty.
    - "ανακοινώθηκε / announced", never "δημοσιευμένη".
    - "έως / until", never "μέχρι".
    - "μέρα", not "ημέρα" (except the set phrase "Εφημερία ημέρας").
    - "τοποθεσία" for the person's location, "θέση" only for a pharmacy's position on the map.
    - "συσκευή / device", never "κινητό".
    - "Καλέστε πριν πάτε. / Call before you go."
    - "καλέστε για το ωράριο / call for the hours" for a duty without printed hours.
    - "Ελέγξτε τη σύνδεση. / Check your connection." for network errors.
    - "Κλείσιμο / Close".
    - "Κοινοποίηση" for share, "Αποστολή" only for sending a report.
  - Accessible names are "Label: {name}" ("Κλήση: {name}", "Κοινοποίηση: {name}"): the pharmacy names are surnames in the nominative, which an article ("στο", "του") would not agree with.
  - Group names: the metro group shows as "Θεσσαλονίκη", not its printed administrative name "Πολεοδομικό Συγκρότημα Θεσσαλονίκης" (`groupDisplayName`, `apps/web/src/lib/groups.ts`).
  - Greek distances are written "μ" and "χλμ".
  - Phone numbers have a space after the area code ("2310 023026").
- **Stale data:** if the newest data is older than 36 hours, show a warning banner. Never hide the data silently.
- **Privacy:** the user's location and medicine searches never leave the device. Distances are computed on the device. The app asks the browser for the position when it opens (the browser asks the person first) and follows it while the page is visible; it does not ask when the person chose an area, cleared the position, or the browser has the permission blocked. Only those choices are stored, as flags, never coordinates. Agreed 5 Oct 2026.
- **Look:** light by default (the owner, 5 Oct 2026). The footer of every page, and the home sheet's footer, offer "Εμφάνιση: Φωτεινή / Σκοτεινή / Αυτόματη"; auto turns dark after sunset in Thessaloniki or whenever the device asks for dark. The choice is remembered on the device (light is not stored). `public/theme.js` runs before the first paint (the CSP allows no inline script), sets `<html data-theme>` and `data-theme-choice`, works out the sun's height at the city centre for auto (`lib/sun.ts`, tested to agree) and checks again every minute; the dark look is a deep green-black with lighter ink, the dark OpenFreeMap base map and the same markers. A pharmacy-green accent (#0a7d45 in the light look) and soft tints for the status colours. The typeface is Manrope (SIL OFL; the owner asked for a change from Commissioner, 5 Oct 2026), self-hosted with its Greek and Latin subsets: plain, very legible Greek at small sizes, tabular figures. On iPhones and iPads the text follows the size set in the system settings (`-apple-system-body`, touch screens only). The header is one 44px row with a small "Φάρμακα" pill for the medicine search, like the language switch. Every icon is Font Awesome Free (CC BY 4.0, credited on the about page), drawn as inline SVG from the icon's path (`components/app/icons.tsx`, `lib/fa.ts`), including the map's markers and controls. Accessibility target: WCAG 2.2 AA; every target at least 44px.
- **Motion:** motion only where it shows where something came from or that something changed: the segmented controls' thumb slides, a tab's panel comes in from its side, rows that stay slide to their new places when the filter, place or time changes (FLIP, the first 12), row details open and close smoothly, the chosen pharmacy's marker drops in with its flag, the sheet settles with a slowing curve and follows a flick, the favourite star and the count pop, the medicine search slides between results and details and fades out. One curve (ease-out-quart, expo for settling), three durations (120, 200, 280 ms), tokens in `global.css`; with reduced motion everything is instant, the map camera included. Agreed 5 Oct 2026.
- **Legal:** "call before you go" in every footer; "not medical advice" on the about page; a privacy page; credits for every data source on the about page; the owner's contact address on the about and privacy pages (`CONTACT_EMAIL` in `apps/web/src/config.ts`).
- **Name:** "pharmacy-skg" is a working title. The public name and domain are chosen before launch.
- **Home layout:** the home screen is an app viewport (D8): it fills exactly the visible screen (`100dvh`, safe areas respected), the page does not scroll, only the sheet's list does, and there is no page footer on it (the sheet ends with a compact one: links, the emergency numbers as 44px call buttons and "Καλέστε πριν πάτε"). Only the header (one row) sits above the map: there is no emergency strip, on any page; the owner found it alarming (5 Oct 2026). On a phone the list comes first: the sheet starts at 70% of the screen, and moving the map lowers it. With no position, the list starts with one row: "Η τοποθεσία μου", "Περιοχή" (a full-screen picker with the field at the top, so the keyboard never covers the areas) and the options button, and at most one short line when the location was refused. The sheet's header holds the tabs, one line with the count and when the data was updated ("Ενημερώθηκε 07:46"), and one toolbar row (the origin chip and the "All / On duty" filter); collapsed, the sheet is exactly as tall as that header. One segmented control everywhere (tabs, filter, "now / another time"). With a position, the nearest pharmacy open now leads the list as the answer, on its status's tint, with labelled "Κλήση" and "Οδηγίες" buttons; pharmacies that close within 30 minutes come after those that stay open (the owner, 5 Oct 2026). The list is a plain list divided by hairlines, not cards: the name (up to two lines, without its legal form, `lib/names.ts`), a short status led by the map's marker ("Εφημερεύει", "Ανοιχτό") with the closing time, and the distance and address, with 46px Call and Directions buttons. Tapping a row opens its details and shows it on the map: favourite ("Αγαπημένο"), share ("Κοινοποίηση") and the pharmacy's page ("Ωράριο") as words with small icons, then the other maps apps (the one chosen is remembered for the Directions button). The origin chip reads "Κοντά μου" when distances are measured from the person's position. Revised several times on 5 Oct 2026 after the owner's iPhone reviews.
- **The chosen pharmacy:** one choice at a time, shown the same way on the row and on the map: the brand's tint with a fine edge on the row; on the map a lifted drop in the pharmacy's colour with a white edge and a small shadow where it meets the ground, a flag above it with the short name and how long it stays open, and the other pins stepping back. Choosing on the map lowers the sheet to a card (name, status, distance, "Κλήση", "Οδηγίες"); pulling it up opens the list at that row, and the cross, a tap on the empty map or Escape let it go. The camera moves only when the pharmacy is not already in the free part of the map, and then shows the origin too when it is close (within 3 km). A chosen area is a ring with its name, not the blue "you are here" dot. Replaced the ring-around-the-pin selection (the owner: "too old, too boring, bad UX", 5 Oct 2026).
- **Map:** MapLibre GL JS with OpenFreeMap tiles (Positron, or its dark style in the dark look) and Greek labels. Pharmacies on duty (and those whose list prints no hours) are never clustered and are drawn above everything else, as the only large markers (a square with a cross); regular hours are small slate dots and extended hours small purple diamonds, kept apart from the blue position dot. Names show from zoom 14.5 for duty pharmacies and only from zoom 17 for the rest; only regular and extended-hours pharmacies are clustered, with small, muted clusters. It starts once the page has settled after the list (about 1–2 s after the list), or at once when touched, so that starting it does not block the first reading and scrolling (apps/web/README.md, Lighthouse).
- **Directions:** deep links into Google Maps, Apple Maps or Waze, plus a call link. No routing API and no travel mode: the maps app keeps the mode the person last used (most people drive at night). The one-tap button opens the app last chosen in a row's details (remembered on the device), otherwise Apple Maps on Apple devices and Google Maps elsewhere.
- **Geocoding:** once per address, with Nominatim. Results are stored with their source and a confidence level, and manual corrections live in the repo. Google geocoding is not used, because of its terms.
- **Tooling:**
  - pnpm workspaces; Vitest; ESLint 10 and Prettier; Playwright later.
  - Strict TypeScript 6. Version 7 is not yet supported by typescript-eslint or `astro check`.
  - Node 22, running `.ts` files natively.
